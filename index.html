Monthly Mentee Progress Check-in

This is your monthly space to honestly reflect on your journey. Tell me what you have learned, what you struggled with, what you accomplished, and where you want to go next. There are no perfect answers here. The goal is to understand your real progress and help you become a more independent and capable developer.

* All questions are required.

Enter the name you normally use during our mentoring sessions.

Tell me what technology, programming concept, framework, language, or development area you have mainly been working on.

Describe the things you actually worked on during this month. You can mention concepts you learned, features you built, exercises you completed, projects you worked on, or anything else that contributed to your development.

Don't just say "a lot" or "a little." Explain what you can do now that you could not do before, what became easier, and what you understand better than you did at the beginning.

Think about the part of your learning that frustrated you, confused you, slowed you down, or made you want to stop. Be specific about what happened.

Explain what you actually did when you could not solve a problem. For example, you might have read documentation, searched for an explanation, debugged the problem, asked someone for help, or tried another approach.

Be honest. This could be lack of consistency, distractions, confusion, lack of time, fear of making mistakes, difficulty understanding something, or another reason.

Think about your habits this month. What would you like to change or do differently so that you can make better progress next month?

Think about your next stage. What specific skill, project, concept, or milestone do you want to reach before our next monthly check-in?

Take a moment to look back at where you started and where you are now. Tell me honestly how you feel about your progress, confidence, struggles, and growth.

Your responses will be recorded privately for your monthly mentoring progress review.
